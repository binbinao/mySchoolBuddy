2. 在平面直角坐标系中, 抛物线 $y=-\frac{3}{5}x^2+bx+c$ 与 $y$ 轴交于点 $A(0,3)$, 与 $x$ 轴的正半轴交于点 $B(5,0)$, 点 $D$ 在线段 $OB$ 上, 且 $OD=1$, 联结 $AD$, 将线段 $AD$ 绕着点 $D$ 顺时针旋转 $90^\circ$, 得到线段 $DE$, 过点 $E$ 作直线 $l \perp x$ 轴, 垂足为 $H$, 交抛物线于点 $F$.


(1) 求这条抛物线的解析式;

(2) 联结 DF, 求 $\cot \angle EDF$ 的值;

(3) 点 G 在直线 l 上, 且 $\angle E D G=45^\circ$, 直接写出点 G 的坐标.

(2) 解: 过点 E 作 EP$\perp$DF 于点 P

$$\begin{align*}\text{(2)}\text{解:}\text{过点} E\text{作} E\text{上}\text{DF}\text{于点} P\\ \because\text{L}\text{AOD}=\text{L}\text{ADE}=\text{LDHE}=90^{\circ}\\ \therefore\text{L}\text{AOD}+\text{LOAD}=90^{\circ}\\ \text{L}\text{AOD}+\text{L}\text{EDH}=90^{\circ}\\ \therefore\text{L}\text{AD}=\text{LEDH}\\ \therefore\text{AD}=\text{DE}\\ \therefore\text{AOD}\text{D}\triangle\text{DHE}\\ \therefore\text{DH}=\text{OA}=3\text{HE}=\text{OD}=1\\ \therefore\text{E}(4,1)\\ \because\text{y}=-\frac{5}{3} x^2+\frac{12}{3} x+3\text{今} x=4, x=4, y=3\\ \therefore\text{F}(4,3)\\ \therefore\text{DH}=\text{HF}=3\\ \therefore\text{L}\text{DFH}=45^{\circ}\\ \therefore\text{EF}=\text{FH}-\text{EH}=3-1=2\\ \therefore\text{F}=\text{PE}=2\times\frac{5}{2}=\sqrt{2}\end{align*}$$

$$\begin{align*}\&\because DF=\sqrt{12} DH=3\sqrt{1}\\ \&\because DP=DF-pF=3\sqrt{12}-\sqrt{2}=2\sqrt{1}\\ \&\because\cot\angle EDF=\frac{DP}{PE}=\frac{2\sqrt{1}}{12}=2\\ \&\because\angle EFD=\angle EDG=45^{\circ}\\ \&\angle FED=\angle DEG\\ \&\therefore\Delta FED=\angle DEG\\ \&\therefore\Delta FED=\angle DEG\\ \&\therefore DE=\sqrt{DH^2+EH^2}=\sqrt{3^2+1^2}=\sqrt{10}\\ \&\text{EF}=\sqrt{FH-HH}=3-1=2\\ \&\therefore EG=\frac{12}{9}=5\\ \&\therefore GH=GE+EH=5+1=6\\ \&\therefore G(4,6)\end{align*}$$

$$\therefore\triangle FED\triangle DEG$$

$$\therefore DE^2=EF\cdot EG$$

$$\because DE=\sqrt{D H^2+EH^2}=\sqrt{3^2+1^2}=\sqrt{10}$$

$$\therefore EG=\frac{12}{9}=5$$

$$\therefore GH=GE+EH=5+1=6$$

$$\therefore G(4,6)$$

② 当点G在点E下方时

$$\because\angle E D G=\angle P F G=45^{\circ}$$

$$\angle DGE=\angle FGD$$

$$\therefore\triangle D E D\wedge\triangle F 6 D$$

$$\therefore DG^2=GE\cdot GF$$

$$\text{设点} G(0, n)$$

$$\therefore DG^2=9+n^2 G E=1-n$$

$$\text{ GF}=3-n$$

$$\therefore q+n^2=(l-n)(3-n)$$

$$\therefore n=-\frac{3}{2}$$

$$\therefore G\left(4,-\frac{3}{2}\right)$$
