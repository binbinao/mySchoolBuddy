1.如图,已知  $\triangle A B C,\angle A=60^{\circ},\angle B=75^{\circ}$  ,将  $\triangle A B C$  绕着点 B顺时针旋转得到  $\triangle A^{\prime} B C^{\prime}$  ,线段  $A^{\prime} C^{\prime}$  与线

段 BC交于 M点,如果  $A^{\prime} B\perp A C$  ,那么  $\frac{C M}{C^{\prime} M}$  的值为  $\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}}{2}$

![A\nB\nM\n第1号\nB\nC\n1\nC\n](2/b7kZFe4oeXwHTPqqda0fYP/figure133d2474b408499ba5b9a2071b2b58b9_0_2.png)
$$\begin{align*}\&\because\angle A=60^{\circ}\quad\angle B=75^{\circ}\\ \&\because\angle C=\angle 3=45^{\circ}\\ \&\because A^{\prime} B\perp A C\\ \&\therefore\angle 1=\angle 2=30^{\circ}\\ \&\text{设} M C^{\prime}=\sqrt{2}\\ \&\because M D=D C^{\prime}=1\\ \&\therefore M B=2\quad B D=\sqrt{3}\\ \&\therefore B C=B C^{\prime}=\sqrt{3}+1\\ \&\therefore C M=\sqrt{3}+1-2=\sqrt{3}-1\\ \&\therefore\frac{C M}{C^{\prime}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}-1}{\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{2}}{2}\end{align*}$$
