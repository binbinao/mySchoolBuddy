1.如图,在  $\triangle A B C$  中,  $\angle A=30^{\circ},\angle B=90^{\circ}, D$  为 A B的中点, E在线段 A C上,  $\frac{A D}{A B}=\frac{D E}{B C}$  ,则 $\frac{A E}{A C}=\frac{1}{2}$  或  $\frac{1}{4}$

![A\nE\n2\nD\nE\n1\nB\nC\n](2/b7kZFe4oeXwHTPqqda0fYP/figure36e456f3a23e4d4290382e98ce0c4fd9_0_1.png)
①$ D E_1// B C$

$$\frac{A D}{A B}=\frac{A E_1}{A C}=\frac{1}{2}$$

②  $D E_1=D E_2$

$\begin{align*}\&\because A=30^{\circ}\quad\angle D E_1 E_2=\angle D E_2 E_1=60^{\circ}\\ \&\therefore D E_2=A E_2=E_1 E_2\\ \&\therefore E_2是 A E_1中点\\ \&\therefore\frac{A E_2}{A C}=\frac{1}{4}\end{align*}$

2.如图,在直角坐标平面内,函数  $y=\frac{m}{x}(x>0, m$  是常数)的图象经过  $A(1,4), B(a, b)$  ,其中  $a>1$  ,过点 A作 x轴垂线,垂足为 C,过点 B作 y轴垂线,垂足为 D,连结  $A D, D C, C B$  .

(1)求  $\triangle A B D$  的面积为 4,求点 B的坐标;

(2)求证:  $DC\parallel AB$  ;

(3)当 A D=B C时,求直线 A B的函数解析式.

![y\nA\nD\nB\nO\nC\nx\n](2/b7kZFe4oeXwHTPqqda0fYP/figure9b1741b7e8574fe9b21f4859912d97d4_0_10.png)
$\begin{align*}\&\text{(2)解:设} B D与 A C交于点 E\\ \&\because A(1,4) B\left(a,\frac{4}{a}\right)\\ &\therefore D\left(0,\frac{4}{a}\right) C(1,0) E\left(1,\frac{4}{a}\right)\\ &\therefore C E=\frac{4}{a}, A E=4-\frac{4}{a}=\frac{4 a-4}{a}\\ &\text{ DE}=1 E B=Q-1\\ &\therefore\frac{C E}{E A}=\frac{D E}{E B}=\frac{1}{a-1}\end{align*}$

(1)解:  $y=\frac{m}{x}$  过点 A(1,4)  $\therefore m=x y=4$   $\because a b=4$   $\because S_{\text{SABD}}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot a(4-b)$   $=2 a-\frac{1}{2} a b$   $=4$   $=2 a-2$   $=4$   $\therefore a=3$   $\therefore b=\frac{4}{3}$   $\therefore B\left(3,\frac{4}{3}\right)$

(3)解:①四边形 A B C D为平行四边形时 $\because C D=A B,\frac{C D}{A B}=\frac{C E}{E A}=\frac{1}{a-1}$ $$\therefore a-1=1$$ $\therefore a=2$  $\therefore B(2,2)$ 设直线  $A B: y=k x+b$ $$\left\{\begin{array}{l} 2 k+b=2\\ k+b=4\end{array}\right.$$ $\left\{\begin{array}{l} k=-2\\ b=5\end{array}\right.$ $$\left\{\begin{array}{l} k=-2\\ b=6\end{array}\right.$$

②四边形ABCD为等腰梯形时  $\therefore A C=B D$  $\therefore 4=a$  $\therefore B(4,1)$ 设直线AB:  $y=k x+b$ $$\left\{\begin{array}{l} 4 k+b=1\\ k+b=4\end{array}\right.$$ $\left\{\begin{array}{l} k=-1\\ b=5\end{array}\right.$  $\therefore y=-x+5$
