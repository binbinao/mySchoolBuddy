$$\begin{align*}\&\because A D=x B C=y\\ \&\text{ OA}=0 B=4\\ \&\therefore C D=x+y-4\sqrt{2}=D E\\ \&\text{ AC}=x-(x+y-4\sqrt{2})\\ \&=4\sqrt{2}-y=B E\\ \&\text{ BD}=y-(x+y-4\sqrt{2})\\ \&=4\sqrt{2}-x\\ \&\because B D^2+B E^2=D E^2\\ \&\therefore\left(4\sqrt{2}-x^2+(4\sqrt{2}-y)^2=(x+y-4\sqrt{2})\right.\\ \&\therefore y=\frac{16}{x}\left(2\sqrt{2}\leq x\leq 4\sqrt{2}\right)\end{align*}$$
